### What we do

We take products from idea to launch, then keep them running.

| **Design** | **Build** | **Run** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| UI / UX design | Web apps & platforms | Cloud hosting & DevOps |
| Websites & landing pages | Mobile apps (iOS & Android) | Integrations & APIs: payments, SMS, maps |
| | Internal systems: ERP, CRM, dashboards | Maintenance & support |
| | E-commerce stores | |

### How we work

- **Fixed-scope project**: a defined scope, a 10-week timeline and a fixed price.
- **Dedicated team**: a monthly team that works on your product as it grows.
- **Support plan**: monthly maintenance and support after launch.
